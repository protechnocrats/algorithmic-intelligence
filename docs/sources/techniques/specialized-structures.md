# Sources for `skills/algorithmic-intelligence/references/techniques/specialized-structures.md`

Where each claim in that file comes from, for maintainers and reviewers. Not part of the installed skill.

- Burton H. Bloom, "Space/Time Trade-offs in Hash Coding with Allowable Errors," *CACM* 13(7), 1970; Bin Fan et al., "Cuckoo Filter: Practically Better Than Bloom," CoNEXT 2014.
- George Varghese and Tony Lauck, "Hashed and Hierarchical Timing Wheels" (1987).
- Flajolet et al., "HyperLogLog" (2007); Graham Cormode and S. Muthukrishnan, "An Improved Data Stream Summary: The Count-Min Sketch" (2005).
- Donald E. Knuth, *The Art of Computer Programming*, Vol. 3, section 6.4 (multiplicative hashing), and Vol. 4A, section 7.1.4 (binary decision diagrams).
- Rasmus Pagh and Flemming Friche Rodler, "Cuckoo Hashing," *Journal of Algorithms* 51(2), 2004.
- Daniel D. Sleator and Robert E. Tarjan, "Self-Adjusting Binary Search Trees," *JACM* 32(3), 1985; Michael L. Fredman and Robert E. Tarjan, "Fibonacci Heaps and Their Uses in Improved Network Optimization Algorithms," *JACM* 34(3), 1987.
- James R. Driscoll, Neil Sarnak, Daniel D. Sleator, and Robert E. Tarjan, "Making Data Structures Persistent" (1989); Phil Bagwell, "Ideal Hash Trees" (2001).
- Jon L. Bentley and James B. Saxe, "Decomposable Searching Problems I: Static-to-Dynamic Transformation," *Journal of Algorithms* 1(4), 1980.
- Adam Kirsch and Michael Mitzenmacher, "Less Hashing, Same Performance: Building a Better Bloom Filter," ESA 2006.
- Berthold Vöcking, "How Asymmetry Helps Load Balancing," *JACM* 50(4), 2003 (d-left hashing); Li Fan et al., "Summary Cache" (2000) (counting Bloom filters).
- Randal E. Bryant, "Graph-Based Algorithms for Boolean Function Manipulation" (1986).
- Pankaj Gupta and Nick McKeown, "Algorithms for Packet Classification," *IEEE Network* 15(2), 2001.
- Eviction policies: Nimrod Megiddo and Dharmendra S. Modha, "ARC: A Self-Tuning, Low Overhead Replacement Cache," FAST 2003; Theodore Johnson and Dennis Shasha, "2Q: A Low Overhead High Performance Buffer Management Replacement Algorithm," VLDB 1994; Gil Einziger, Roy Friedman, and Ben Manes, "TinyLFU: A Highly Efficient Cache Admission Policy," *ACM Transactions on Storage* 13(4), 2017.
- William Pugh, "Skip Lists: A Probabilistic Alternative to Balanced Trees," *CACM* 33(6), 1990; Raimund Seidel and Cecilia R. Aragon, "Randomized Search Trees," *Algorithmica* 16(4–5), 1996 (treaps); Pedro Celis, *Robin Hood Hashing*, PhD thesis, University of Waterloo, 1986.
- Peter van Emde Boas, R. Kaas, and E. Zijlstra, "Design and Implementation of an Efficient Priority Queue," *Mathematical Systems Theory* 10(1), 1977; Dan E. Willard, "Log-Logarithmic Worst-Case Range Queries Are Possible in Space Θ(N)," *Information Processing Letters* 17(2), 1983 (y-fast tries).
